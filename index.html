📋 Tourist Guide

🔹Ladies and gentlemen, I have a proposal.

Let's go on a group tour along the Georgian Military Road. Not for those who love loungers by the pool, but for those who understand:
The Caucasus is not just a resort, it is a state of soul and stomach.


Route:
The Georgian Military Road is rightly considered one of the most picturesque routes of the Caucasus. Its history goes back to ancient times: this path appeared long before a modern asphalt highway was laid here. Even in antiquity, a busy trade route passed through the Darial Gorge, connecting the southern regions of the Caucasus with the North Caucasus and Eastern Europe.

 

Zhinvali. Water. Lots of water. Beautiful. Photos. Let's move on.

Ananuri. Fortress. Stones. Wind. The feeling that you are in the 12th century, but with an iPhone. The guide will tell you how people lived here, and you will think: "They lived quite well."

Gudauri. Mountains. Skiing (if in season). Shashlik (always). Altitude 2196 m. Your "self" at this altitude may behave inadequately (this is normal).

Kazbegi. The finale. Tsminda Sameba Church. Kazbek. If you're lucky, you'll see the summit. If not, you'll see a cloud that is almost Kazbek.

 

Tour features:

— A driver who takes the serpentine as if he was born with a steering wheel in his hands. You may get carsick. It's part of the authenticity.

— A guide who will tell you about Zhinvali, Ananuri and Gudauri, but you will still forget which is which. That's normal.

— The group. You will laugh, argue, take photos and maybe drink chacha together on the roadside. That's the Caucasus.

 

What to bring:

— Warm clothes (the mountains don't ask what your weather forecast is).

— Good mood (buy the rest on site).

— A stomach ready for khinkali (it won't be ready, but you'll manage).

 

P.S. If after the tour you can't pronounce "Gudauri", you did everything right.

🔹Friends, I have an idea for one day!

If you haven't seen the Martvili canyons and the Prometheus Cave. Let's go!

Let's hurry to Kutaisi — to see canyons and caves that take your breath away (and make your legs a bit weak from the stairs).

 

Route:

The road from Tbilisi to Kutaisi is not a "road" but a tasting menu of Georgia in one day: a sip of history in Mtskheta, a spoonful of drama in Gori, a pinch of mysticism in Ubisa, a portion of greens at the pass, and a full main course in Kutaisi. You will arrive not "tired from the road" but "overloaded with impressions". And that's the best kind of tiredness.

 

Martvili Canyon. Turquoise water, boat, rocks. You will understand why Georgians are so proud of their nature. And why the guide says: "Look, but don't lean too far."

 

Prometheus Cave. An underground world with lighting that makes you want to cry with delight. Or because you forgot your jacket. In the cave it's +14, so yes, bring the jacket.

 

Kutaisi. The finale. Bagrati Cathedral, old town, khachapuri. You will be tired but happy. Or just tired. But with photos.

 

Tour features:
— A driver who knows the road better than you know your neighborhood.
— A guide who will tell you about canyons, caves and Prometheus. You will forget half of it but will nod.
— The group. You will laugh, take photos and search together for where to eat. That's Georgian hospitality.

 

What to bring:
— Comfortable shoes (this is not advice, it's a requirement).
— A jacket (it's cold in the cave, even if it's hot in Tbilisi).
— Water (lots).
— Patience (there will be many stairs).
— Good mood (buy the rest on site).


P.S. If after the tour you can't pronounce "Martvili", you did everything right. If you can — then you're not tired yet. But that won't last long.
